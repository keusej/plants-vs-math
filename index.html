📱 iPhone Safari Fullscreen Guide

How to hide the tab bar & address bar for a borderless gaming experience:

🌟 Method 1: Add to Home Screen (Recommended!)

Installs the game as a native standalone app with zero browser bars, no tabs, and full edge-to-edge screen:

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow ⎋) at the bottom/top.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  4. Open "Plants vs Math" from your home screen!
⚡ Method 2: Hide Toolbar & Tabs in Safari
  • Hide Address Bar: Tap the "aA" button in Safari's address bar ➔ select "Hide Toolbar".
  • Hide Landscape Tab Row: Open iPhone Settings ➔ Safari ➔ under Tabs, switch "Landscape Tab Bar" to OFF.
